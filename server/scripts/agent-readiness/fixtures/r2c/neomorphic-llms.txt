## Reliance boundaries

- A record signature proves origin and integrity, not truth beyond the record's stated method and scope.
- Check observation time, limitations, the current static publication, expiry, supersession, and any later correction or revocation record before relying on any record.
- Publication state is explicit. Research and preview material is not represented as a stable production service.
- No A2A agent endpoint, MCP server, MPP payable discovery document, live x402 payable route, agents.neomorphic.io gateway, or api.neomorphic.io product origin is currently advertised.
- Catalog presence, a payment challenge, or a public record is not evidence of adoption, settlement, or demand.
