# Discovery and the record

Discovery is not authorization, settlement,
demand, or revenue. Machine entry links describe the next usable action and
its price. The internal absence corpus keeps this boundary here so customer
pages can sell without carrying our accounting vocabulary.

Retain the original absence fixtures and their accept/reject decisions. A
missing source, unavailable feed or unchanged scoped record cannot supply a
positive demand observation. A browser journey event is diagnostic; the
existing payment and receipt stores remain financial authority.
