## What “insufficient” means

MONITOR-STATUS (wave monitor closeout ~18:55Z) quotes:

> F07 `harness_fixture` labelled insufficient

Evidence labelled `harness_fixture` is **not** customer evidence and **not** production evidence. It is a local/harness observation: a disposable fixture, SAMPLE, or operator walkthrough. Labelling it insufficient means a later reader must not treat it as:

- a customer-owned case,
- hosted/production correspondence,
- paid demand, settlement, or adoption,
- a substitute for a distinct customer/production acceptance event.

Public Neo copy on the outside-operator first-job surface states the same boundary in different words: a local fixture is not hosted correspondence; a synthetic requester is not a customer; a delivered artifact is not accepted until a distinct acceptance event.
