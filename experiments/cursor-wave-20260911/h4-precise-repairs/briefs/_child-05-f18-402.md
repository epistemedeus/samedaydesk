## Regression

Treating a 402 row as `success: true` or `outcome: "success"` must fail.

`assert402IsNotSuccess(row)` returns `{ ok: false, rejected: true, code: "unpaid-402-is-not-success", outcome: "unpaid-held" }`.

`classifyProbe` maps 402 → `unpaid-held`. This pack does not assert that `200` + `paid: true` happened and does not invent a live paid settlement.
