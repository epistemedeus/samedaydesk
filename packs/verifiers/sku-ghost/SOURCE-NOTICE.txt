SKU-ghost verifier
==================

Owning repo: epistemedeus/samedaydesk
Write boundary: packs/verifiers/sku-ghost/**

This pack is a read-only oracle over already-committed SameDayDesk SKU
surfaces:

- server/pricing.js          (authoritative offer slug → cents)
- client/src/lib/services.ts (homepage catalog + Stripe Payment Link keys)
- client/public/llms.txt     (named dollar lines in Start here)

It does not edit prices or SKUs. It does not touch checkout, Stripe,
x402 payment, or neomorphic-io. It does not publish a catalog.

A ghost SKU is an advertised slug (or named dollar line) that is missing
from server/pricing.js, or advertised at a cents value that does not
match that map. Empty advertised catalogs are silent_empty_success, not
"no ghosts".
