Pack: work-terms-dataset
Role: maintained public work-terms dataset (versions, invalidation, attribution, republication rights)
Dataset: data/work-terms
Pin: PIN.json (liveScrape false, universalCoverage false)

This pack maintains operator-supplied metadata about named public work-platform
terms. It does not scrape. It does not fetch private, auth-gated, dashboard,
or NDA terms. Ingest of a private-terms scrape is refused (code
private_terms_scrape).

Coverage is named-source only. The catalog lists coverage gaps. The pack
does not claim a complete or universal corpus of work terms.

Republication rights are explicit on every record. Full terms bodies are
never stored. Commercial resale of third-party terms is refused.

Invalidation is first-class: an invalidated record is not current and must
not be republished.

Disjoint from H04 licensed regression packs (no hidden evaluator answers,
gold labels, or licensed eval fixtures).
