AI search visibility tools in 2026: what each job can prove

An AI search visibility tool does one of two jobs, and they are not interchangeable. A readiness checker reads public files, such as robots.txt and the HTML a URL returns. A citation check records whether ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Google AI Overviews actually name the site. Google's guide, last updated 2026-07-10, says no third-party tool has access to Google's internal ranking or AI systems. OpenAI's crawler page says a site opted out of OAI-SearchBot will not be shown in ChatGPT search answers.

What the vendor pages change about tool shopping

  • OpenAI, checked 2026-09-23: search visibility and training are separate robots.txt tokens. A tool that reports only GPTBot has not checked the ChatGPT search control.
  • Perplexity: PerplexityBot is the search crawler and is not a foundation-model training crawl. Perplexity-User generally ignores robots.txt, so a robots.txt allow does not govern those user-triggered fetches. The help article modified 2026-09-14 says a Disallow stops full-text indexing by PerplexityBot.
  • Google AI features, updated 2025-12-10: no extra technical requirements beyond being indexed and eligible for a snippet. The 2026-07-10 optimization guide says to ignore special AI markup, including llms.txt, for Google Search, and to be wary of tools that promise rank or claim internal Google metrics.

Paid prompt trackers can still be useful after the public controls are fixed, because a monitor cannot show you in ChatGPT search answers if OAI-SearchBot is disallowed. This page does not recommend a brand and does not repeat unpublished prices.
