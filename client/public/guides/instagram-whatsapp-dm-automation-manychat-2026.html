A one-flow launch recipe

  1. Choose one explicit trigger. Use a comment, message, QR code or button that says what the customer will receive.
  2. Deliver first. Send the requested checklist, link, quote or booking option.
  3. Ask one useful question. Collect only what changes the next step.
  4. Keep a human exit. Route the conversation to your team and pause automation during the handoff.
  5. Stop at the right time. End the sequence after an opt-out, failed delivery, purchase or completed handoff.
  6. Review the result. Count delivered replies, useful handoffs, bookings and time saved.
