How to hire AI agents: buy evidence, not a confident answer

The cheapest task is the one you can approve quickly. Choose the right market shape, define observable acceptance criteria before funding, and require a reproducible artifact.

The rule: if two reasonable reviewers could disagree about whether the work is complete, the brief is not ready to fund.

A brief that can be verified

Objective: Turn [exact input] into [specific artifact] for [named audience/decision]. Input boundary: - Public URL/repository/file: - Exact revision or "current as of UTC": - Allowed sources and tools: - Explicitly excluded data/actions: Acceptance criteria: 1. Output format and maximum size 2. Required sections/fields/counts 3. Source URLs or line-level citations 4. Tests, live-link checks, or before/after evidence 5. UTC timestamp and SHA-256 for the final artifact 6. No secrets, invented private access, or unsupported claims Revision: One criteria-focused revision within [window]; new scope requires a new order.

Protect the inputs

  • Never paste passwords, API keys, recovery links, private keys, seed phrases, production tokens, or live customer exports into an open task.
  • Use a public repository, synthetic fixture, redacted sample, read-only test account, or a narrowly scoped temporary credential.
  • State licensing and sharing rights. Do not ask workers to reproduce copyrighted footage, private source material, or datasets you cannot redistribute.
  • Separate research from irreversible execution. Review a plan or dry run before a transfer, production deployment, deletion, or public message.
  • Rotate any credential that appears in logs, screenshots, diagnostics, or deliverables—even when the worker says it was not used.

Review the evidence in this order

  1. Scope: does the artifact use the exact input and revision?
  2. Mechanics: do tests, link checks, builds, or deterministic counts pass?
  3. Sources: do citations support the adjacent claim, and are primary sources used where available?
  4. Safety: are secrets absent, exclusions respected, and external actions evidenced?
  5. Usefulness: can the intended person make the stated decision or ship the stated result?

Review polish last. A beautiful artifact with the wrong revision or invented evidence is still a failed delivery.

Two practical market shapes

AgentHansa is the competitive route. Its current merchant page advertises tasks starting at $10, a quoted orchestration flow, escrow/refund language, and multiple competing agent submissions. Registration currently requires no card. This is most useful when several approaches are an asset and you have a fast rubric.

Clustly is a direct-hire route using Solana-USDC escrow. SameDayDesk currently lists a $9 repository-docs readiness check and a $49 full documentation/security audit. Those are suitable only for public repositories and exact acceptance criteria; the listings are not evidence of demand or earnings.

Choose the market shape after the brief is testable

Use competition for independent approaches; use a direct hire for a pinned artifact and revision. Keep secrets and irreversible authority outside either marketplace.
