Methods

This page exists so that a buyer, a competitor, or a journalist can check how a number on one of our reports was produced, and can produce it themselves. If a claim we make is not reproducible from this page, treat it as marketing rather than measurement.

Two batteries, and only one of them can carry a refund

Every panel question falls into one of two batteries, and they are scored differently because they are different kinds of question.

BatteryWhat it asksWhat it can support
A: facts Name, address, phone, hours, whether you are open, which services you sell, what you charge. Binary right or wrong scoring, and therefore refund and acceptance rules. A fact is checkable against your own canonical record.
B: context Best in a category, who to pick between two names, what reviews say. Context only. Reported and quoted, never scored as pass or fail, never attached to a refund. These answers move for reasons no vendor controls.

This split is why our refund classes never mention citations or rankings. Battery B is where those live, and Battery B does not carry promises.

How a run is taken

  • Logged out. No account history, no personalization carried into the answer.
  • A fresh session per question. Temporary or private, so an earlier answer cannot prime a later one.
  • One geography, stated. The market you gave us in the intake, held constant across the run.
  • K equals 3. Every question is asked three times. A fact that appears in one run of three is not a finding, and it is not a fix either.
  • Cited sources captured every run. Where the answer shows sources, they are recorded with the answer, because the source is the thing we can actually change.
  • Never blended. Each engine is reported on its own. There is no combined score, because averaging two engines produces a number that describes neither.

Defect words

An answer gets one of these, and the words come from how owners already describe the problem.

WordMeaning
rightThe answer matches your canonical record.
staleThe answer was true once. A price you changed, a service you retired, hours that moved.
mixedThe answer belongs to another business, or blends you with one. Reviews that are not yours, an address that is not yours.
inventedThe answer has no source we can find, including in your own history. A phone number nobody ever published.
silentYou are not named at all on a question where naming would be expected.

There is no numeric score anywhere in this method. A count of defects by word, per engine, per run, is the whole reporting shape.

The frozen question panel

The same questions run for every client. Only the slots change, and the slots come from your intake rather than from our reading of your website.

SlotFilled with
BUSINESSYour legal or trading name, plus the name buyers actually say
CATEGORYThe noun a buyer types, not your internal service-menu wording
CITYYour primary market
SERVICEOne flagship service you actually sell
COMPETITORThe name buyers compare you with
OLD_CITYYour previous market, if you moved. Adds one scored question rather than replacing one.

The free report asks five of these questions once, on one surface: identity, status and hours, service truth, a head to head, and price. The paid audit asks the full panel across named engines with three runs each. Both print the exact question text on the card, so you can see what was asked rather than trusting a summary of it.

Engines, and what can honestly be said about each

Correction speed is not one number. It depends on how the surface gets its answer, so we publish a sentence per surface instead of a blended promise.

SurfaceWhat we can say
Business listings and mapsListing facts can usually be corrected quickly. That is not the same as the answer above the listing changing.
Google AI overviewsWe correct the sources it reads. Simple facts often move in a few weeks. A mix-up with another business is a different and harder problem.
ChatGPT with search onCan move within about a week when it cites your page. We measure it and we only make commitments about this case.
ChatGPT with search offWaits for a model update. Nobody can date that, so we do not.
PerplexityFastest when it is already reading your page. We check the citation card, not only the sentence.
GeminiThe app and the search surface behave like two products. The app often does not look at your site at all.
OthersReported when a client is already being misquoted there. We publish no correction timeline for a surface we have not measured.

What the free report does not do

Five questions, once, on one surface, with mechanical labels only: whether your brand was named, and whether an answer carries a fact you told us should be true. It does not assign a defect word, because that is a judgement and a human makes it in the paid audit. It stores one live run per domain per day, and a repeat visit shows that stored copy with its timestamp.

Every card on the free panel names the exact surface that produced it, in the form of the API and model used, with the time. A consumer chat session can answer differently from an API call, and the card says so rather than implying it is a screenshot of a consumer product.

Limits we will not paper over

  • Answers vary between runs. That is why K equals 3 and why a single screenshot proves nothing.
  • An answer with no cited source cannot be traced to a page, and we label the source as suspected rather than confirmed.
  • Nobody controls what a model outputs. Every commitment we make is about artifacts and measurements, never about a sentence an engine will produce.
  • The sample is a structured panel, not a census of everything anyone could ask.

The same method, run against our own production site and published while the findings were still live, is the self-audit. The rules that turn these measurements into money are in the terms.
