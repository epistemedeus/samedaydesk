SameDayDesk work-terms dataset coverage

This dataset covers only the named public sources below. It is not a complete or universal corpus of work terms.

Included platforms (named-source only):
- gofrantic (charter; no formal /terms page on observation date)
- taskmarket (draft terms + draft acceptable use)
- bountybook (public terms)
- moltjobs (public terms)
- superteam (public agent skill/rules; site /terms 404 on observation date)
- agoragentic (public terms; listing harvest forbidden)
- samedaydesk (first-party /terms stub, invalidated as not operative)

Known gaps (not an attack surface, not completeness):
- taskforce: no public Terms; task inventory is API-key gated
- worq: no Terms or robots found on 2026-08-08 check
- every other work platform, marketplace, employer, or contractor ToS not named above

This pack does not scrape. Private, auth-gated, dashboard, NDA, and
employee-only terms are refused on ingest.

Disjoint from H04 licensed regression packs.
